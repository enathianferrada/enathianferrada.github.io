About me

Education. Undergraduate student at Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María. I study the cold molecular gas content and host galaxy properties of nearby X-ray AGN with ionised outflows, focusing on the morphology and kinematics of these galaxies.

Research interests. My work deals mainly with the molecular gas content of Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN).
